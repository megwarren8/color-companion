What do these numbers mean?
ΔEHow far apart two colors are. Under 1 is a difference almost nobody sees side by side, 2 to 5 is a nudge, 10 is clearly a different color, 20 is obviously different.
Perfect / Excellent / Good / Fair / LooseThe same number in words: under 2, under 5, under 10, under 18, and beyond.
3 swatches, spread 12.6Three different people swatched that marker and their readings sit up to 12.6 ΔE apart. A big spread means real markers vary (batch, paper, how hard you press), so trust the family more than the exact ranking. Hover any badge for the full sentence.
measured onceOne person's swatch, with nothing to cross-check it.
not measured yetNobody has swatched it. The color is the maker's chart, nudged by how far the rest of that set turned out to be from the chart.
too close to callThe next match is within 0.7 ΔE, so which one is "first" carries no real information. Pick whichever you own.
in my collectionYou already own this one, from the scan of your markers.
